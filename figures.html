The Shape of Myth

Across 926 catalogue traditions and 2,138 recorded motifs, exact-motif and semantic-topic networks preserve a shared global backbone while reorganizing local communities.

The central finding

The two partitions agree more than expected under both fixed-margin and macroarea-restricted null models, but their agreement is partial rather than interchangeable. Binary community B1 is principally divided between topic communities T2 and T4, showing that the operational definition of “similarity” changes the local structure recovered from the same archive.
