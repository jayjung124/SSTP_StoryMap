The B1 reorganization is geographically patterned

Among B1 entries, B1∩T4 (n=148) is more geographically concentrated: 90 records (60.8%) have catalogue locations in Amazonia and Northern South America. B1∩T2 (n=162) is distributed across several macroareas. This pattern does not establish origin, migration, borrowing, or independent recurrence.

Scope and uncertainty. The chart uses the 310 B1 records in the two principal split cells—not all of T2 (n=204) or all of T4 (n=164). The 12×2 composition association is large descriptively (χ²=94.53, df=11; Cramér’s V=0.552). Residual magnitudes near or above 2 are exploratory cell diagnostics, not multiplicity-corrected tests. Recorded-motif counts also differ (means 35.3 vs 61.1), so geography, catalogue coverage, and representation remain entangled.
