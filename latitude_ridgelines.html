Spatial distribution · Figure 7

The partitions occupy different latitude bands

These ridgelines summarize the latitude of all catalogue reference points in each community. In the topic partition, T2 is broadly distributed while T4 is concentrated nearer the equator. This is a spatial association with the classification, not a history of transmission.

Partition

How it is calculated. Each ridge is a Gaussian kernel density estimate using the selected bandwidth. All ridges share one density scale, so a narrower distribution can form a taller peak. The dashed line marks the equator. The points are catalogue reference locations; they are not origins, homelands, or complete areas of cultural practice.
