Contextual audit · Figure 6

Modern climate context differs across the topic partition

At their catalogue reference locations, the full T2 community (n=204) has a mean of 21.1°C and 1,497 mm annual precipitation; the full T4 community (n=164) averages 23.5°C and 2,043 mm. These present-day covariates contextualize the classifications; they do not reconstruct the climate of a story’s formation.
