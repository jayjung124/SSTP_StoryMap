How to read the evidence. ARI and AMI are chance-adjusted agreement measures; NMI is normalized but not chance-adjusted; VI is a distance, so lower values indicate closer partitions. z-Rand is an unbounded standardized pair-count statistic, not a percentage or effect size. Across all 428,275 entry pairs, 55,389 are grouped together by both partitions. Under a macroarea-restricted null, mean ARI is 0.2075; observed ARI remains higher (z=17.52, p=0.00001), so broad geography explains part—but not all—of the agreement.
